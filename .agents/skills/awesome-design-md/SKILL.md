## 🛠️ How to Use a Brand Design System

1. Identify the aesthetic that best matches your target audience or product brief.
2. Read the corresponding `design-md/<brand>/DESIGN.md` specification file.
3. Apply its exact tokens:
   - **Color hierarchy**: Primary background, foreground, surface elevations, accent colors, and border contrasts.
   - **Typography**: Heading font families, font weights, tracking/letter-spacing, and line-height scales.
   - **Borders & Radii**: Corner radius scales (`rounded-lg`, `rounded-2xl`, etc.) and border opacities (`border-white/10`, `border-black/5`).
   - **Elevation & Shadows**: Shadow recipes, frosted glass blur intensities (`backdrop-blur-md`), and glow gradients.
   - **Motion & Micro-interactions**: Hover states, transition timing functions (`cubic-bezier`), and active spring states.
